Privacy Policy – AgentJ YouTube

Last updated: November 3, 2026

AgentJ YouTube is a personal tool operated by and for a single owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos the owner chooses to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • youtube.readonly — to read back the owner's channel and video status (for example, whether a scheduled video is public).

How data is used and stored

Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties. No data from Google APIs is used for advertising. AgentJ YouTube's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
